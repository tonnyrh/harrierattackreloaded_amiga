# Carrier graphics reference

Generated with the built-in image generator on 2026-09-17, using the previous
16x8 frontal Harrier as reference. The sheet requests a brighter cockpit, a
compact grey naval twin-barrel AA gun, and a left-facing heavy bomber.
`carrier-weapons-reference.png` is the source sheet; the three cropped RGBA
sources are retained here.

Production masters remain indexed 16-colour OCS PNGs at 8x8 (gun) or 16x8
(aircraft), with transparent index zero. Conversion uses alpha-aware BOX
downsampling and the project palette, restricting guns to neutral pens. The
front aircraft keeps green source hues in the green palette family. No
background-colour flood removal is applied to the final masters. Validation
reports are adjacent; runtime banks are generated by pack-enhanced-graphics.py.

The side Harrier silhouette is retained from original native pixel arrays;
three cockpit pixels use bright sprite pen 6 in Enhanced mode. Classical art
remains unchanged. Mirrored guns and aircraft are packed offline.
